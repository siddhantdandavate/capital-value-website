UAE Golden Visa Property Guidance

Secure long-term 10-year UAE residency through strategic property acquisitions and structured mortgage financing in Dubai.

Eligibility Criteria

AED 2,000,000 Property Threshold

Under current UAE government guidelines, investors acquiring property valued at AED 2,000,000 or more are eligible to apply for a renewable 10-year residency visa for themselves and their immediate family.

Crucially, properties purchased with mortgage financing are fully eligible, provided minimum equity conditions and official bank NOC letters are satisfied.

Key Benefits of the 10-Year Golden Visa

  • ✓ 10-Year renewable residency with zero requirement for an employer sponsor
  • ✓ Unlimited time spent outside the UAE without invalidating your residency status
  • ✓ Sponsorship of spouse, children of any age, and domestic staff
  • ✓ Seamless access to UAE banking, driving licenses, and healthcare
  • ✓ Can be applied across single or multiple freehold properties
